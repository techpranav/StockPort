# Opportunity Radar

The Opportunity Radar is the **primary canvas** in Discover.

## What it encodes

The radar maps each opportunity using:

- **Score/confidence** (strength)
- **Liquidity/volume** (tradability)
- **Volatility** (risk/variance proxy)

## What to look for

- Candidates with **high score + high liquidity**
- Candidates that cluster (potential regime/sector theme)

## Typical workflow

1. Scan the radar visually to pick 3–5 candidates.
2. Use filters to narrow noise (confidence, liquidity, strategy).
3. Open the signal stream cards for details, then move to Decide.
