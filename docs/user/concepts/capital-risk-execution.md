# Capital, Risk, and Execution

## Capital

Capital determines how many trades can be taken and how much risk can be allocated.
When available, the system tracks:

- **Total** capital
- **Available** capital
- **Allocated** capital
- **Reserved** capital

## Risk

Risk is the system’s constraint layer. In practice it answers:

- *How much can I lose on this trade?*
- *How does this trade affect portfolio risk?*

In Stockport’s UI:

- **Decide** focuses on risk–reward for a candidate.
- **Review** surfaces exposure and drawdown concepts.

## Execution

Execution is operational performance:

- **Fill rate**
- **Latency**
- **Slippage**

**Execute** is designed to be calm and minimal so you can monitor outcomes without noise.
