# Confidence & Market Regime

## Algo confidence

**Algo confidence** is a top-level readiness signal derived from strategy performance.

- It is designed to answer: *“How aligned is the system with current conditions?”*
- In Insight it is the **hero element**.

### How to use it

- **High**: prioritize Discover candidates; the system is likely operating in its “comfort zone”.
- **Medium**: be selective; validate candidates via Decide before acting.
- **Low**: treat candidates as low conviction; confirm system health and regime mismatch risks.

## Market regime

Market regime is a simplified label for the current environment (trend/chop/risk-on/risk-off).

Insight exposes regime-related descriptors such as:

- **Regime** (bullish/bearish/neutral)
- **Volatility state**
- **Breadth state**

### Why regime matters

Many strategies are regime-sensitive:

- Trend-following can degrade in sideways markets.
- Mean reversion can degrade in strong trends.

Use regime context to interpret candidates rather than reading signals in isolation.
