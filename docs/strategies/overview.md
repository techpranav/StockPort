# Strategies (Overview)

## What is a strategy in Stockport?

A **strategy** is a repeatable set of conditions that identifies potential entries (and sometimes exits). Strategies:

- Observe market data (candles + volume)
- Compute indicators/patterns
- Emit **signals** when conditions match

## How strategies are used

1. **Detect**: Strategy produces a signal.
2. **Explain**: Signal is enriched with the “why”.
3. **Decide**: Risk/capital constraints approve/reject.
4. **Execute**: If approved, an order may be placed and tracked.

## Included strategies

- Breakout
- Mean reversion
- Momentum
- Support/Resistance bounce
- Swing trading
- Trend following
