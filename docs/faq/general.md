# FAQ (General)

## What is Stockport?

Stockport is a trading analysis system that scans markets, generates signals, and helps you make capital-aware, risk-aware decisions.

## Is Stockport fully automated?

It can be, but the recommended progression is:

1. Manual execution
2. Paper/simulated execution
3. Live execution (only after you trust the full pipeline)

## Why do I see fewer signals today?

Common reasons:

- Provider health is degraded (YELLOW/RED)
- Your universe/filters are restrictive
- Market regime changed (range → trend or vice versa)
