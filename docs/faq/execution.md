# FAQ (Execution)

## Why was an order rejected?

Typical causes:

- Broker authentication/configuration issue
- Risk limits blocked the decision
- Insufficient capital or position-size constraints
- Market closed / symbol not tradable

## Can I use Stockport without live trading?

Yes. You can use it purely for scanning + decision support, and place trades manually.

## How do I troubleshoot execution issues?

- Check provider health first (bad data can lead to bad decisions)
- Check broker configuration
- Use logs and the execution timeline to see where it failed
