# FAQ (Strategies)

## Can multiple strategies trigger on the same symbol?

Yes. Stockport can surface multiple strategy signals for the same symbol. Use **confidence**, **regime fit**, and **risk–reward** to prioritize.

## Why does the same strategy behave differently across days?

Because market context changes:

- Volatility regime changes
- Index/sector leadership changes
- Liquidity varies intraday

## Should I enable all strategies?

Not recommended at the beginning. Start with 1–2 strategies that match your style and learn their failure modes.
