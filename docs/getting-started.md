# Getting Started with Stockport (New User Guide)

This guide is written for **new users**: it explains what to click, what to look at first, and how to use Stockport safely.

!!! tip
    If you’re setting up Stockport on a new machine, switch to **Developer docs** from the top navigation.

## What Stockport does (in one minute)

Stockport helps you go from *market noise* to *actionable decisions*:

1. **Scanner** finds candidates (opportunities)
2. **Strategies** evaluate candidates and emit **signals**
3. **Decision engine** applies risk + capital rules to approve/reject
4. **Execution** (optional) places orders and tracks fills

## Recommended mode for new users

- Start with **Manual execution**
- Use a **small symbol universe**
- Enable **1–2 strategies** and learn their behavior

## First-time walkthrough (5–10 minutes)

### 1) Open Insight (health check)

In **Insight**:

- Confirm provider health is **GREEN**
- Confirm the system clock/time is correct
- Confirm the market regime is reasonable (trend/range)

### 2) Open Discover (find candidates)

In **Discover**:

- Watch the opportunity/signal stream
- Filter to the strategies you want to learn first
- Open a signal and read the explainability (“why”)

### 3) Open Decide (risk–reward first)

In **Decide**:

- Validate entry/stop/target
- Ensure risk–reward looks good
- Ensure position size is sensible for your capital

### 4) Open Execute (only if you are ready)

In **Execute**:

- Place the order (manual/paper/live depending on your configuration)
- Monitor status changes and fills

### 5) Open Review (learn and iterate)

In **Review**:

- Track outcomes
- Identify what worked (and under which regime)
